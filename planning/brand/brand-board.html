fettle

To fettle is to trim the rough edges off a casting until it is finished and fit to use. In fine fettle means in good condition. That is the whole job of the app.

What we are

A calm, plain-spoken workshop for your PC. It finds what is clogging it up, tells you in ordinary words what each thing is, and does nothing until you say so. Everything it does can be undone.

Plain EnglishSay what a thing is and what happens if it goes. No jargon, no scare numbers.
Nothing behind your backShow it first. Act only on what the person ticks. Never run a surprise scan.
Undo anythingSwitched off, moved, cleaned: each step says how to reverse it.

Who it is for

People who own a Windows PC and are not sure what is safe to remove. The parent whose laptop got slow, the gamer whose C: drive is full, the person who has been burnt by a cleaner that deleted something. They want a competent friend, not a dashboard.

Why this name

Short, easy to say and spell, no "PC" or "optimizer" (generic words that cannot be owned, and a trademark record for PCOPTIMIZER already turns up in search). The meaning is quietly specific: finishing and conditioning, not "boosting" or "cleaning up". Clearance check is still open, see the plan.

The mark

A honey shaving curling off a clean board. The board is your drive or your PC; the curl is the rough stuff coming away. It is a simple shape that survives at 16 pixels, and it does not depend on the letter F, so the name can change without redrawing it.

Rules for the mark

Keep clear space of one board-height around it. Never recolour the curl except to a single colour, never add shine or a gradient, never rotate it. Honey is only for the curl and the current tab: it has too little contrast to be text or a thin line on light backgrounds.

Colour

Kept from the app you already know, now with names and rules. Plum and cream carry the page, teal is the working colour, and honey is the spark, used sparingly. Teal has two strengths: the brand teal for icons and large shapes, and a deeper teal for anything that has to be read.

Type

Two families with different jobs. Bricolage Grotesque has the character of a hand-cut sign: it carries the name and headlines. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next was designed by the Braille Institute so that every letter is unmistakable. It carries everything people have to read and act on, which suits an app about being clear.

Keep your PC in fine fettle.
Bricolage Grotesque, ExtraBold and Bold, tight tracking

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, Regular and Bold, normal tracking. Sentence case everywhere. Disk sizes read 1.4 GB, folders read C:\Users\you\Downloads, and nothing ever shouts in capitals.

Both are open source (SIL OFL) and ship as variable fonts through @fontsource, so they can be bundled in the app with no network request. Today's app uses Nunito.

Voice

A capable friend who has seen this before. Calm, concrete and a little dry. Says what happened and what to do next. Never apologises, never panics, never sells.
