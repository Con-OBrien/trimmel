# Name shortlist, round 2

**Decision (2026-10-22): Trimmel.** Official trademark checks are still open; see the last section.

Date: 2026-10-22. Fettle is out: a live FETTLE trademark record exists in US Class 42 (software design and development services), and the main domains are taken.

## What I can and cannot promise

I cannot prove a name is "not trademarked anywhere". Nobody can from a web search. What I did, for every name below:

1. Checked whether the `.com` and `.app` domains have name servers (a quick DNS test; "free" means no name servers, not "available to buy").
2. Searched the web, including trademark listing sites, for the name with "trademark", "software" and "app".
3. Looked for sound-alikes in software, since similar-sounding names also count in a trademark dispute.

A name that passes this is a **candidate**, not a clearance. The finalist still needs the official searches in the last section before any money is spent on artwork, a site or a launch.

## Candidates

### 1. Trimmel (lowest legal risk)

- **Idea:** "trim", plus "mel", the Latin for honey (our brand colour). A coined word, so it is easy to protect and easy to find in search.
- **Say it:** TRIM-el. One obvious spelling.
- **Checks:** no active trademark found. The only near hit is TRIMEL CORPORATION, filed 1990 and cancelled in 2001. `trimmel.com` and `trimmel.app` both looked unregistered.
- **Watch:** Trimmel is also a surname (it will return people and small firms in a search), and "Trimel" is a sound-alike worth a proper look.
- **Fit with the mark:** good. Trimming is what the shaving does.
- **Tagline that fits:** "Trim the clutter. Keep the speed."

### 2. Roomier (best meaning)

- **Idea:** "more room", which is the benefit in one word. Friendly and instantly understood.
- **Say it:** ROOM-ee-er.
- **Checks:** no trademark or software product found under this name. `roomier.app` and `roomier.io` looked unregistered; `.com` is taken.
- **Watch:** it is an everyday comparative adjective, so it is weak as a trademark (the office may call it descriptive for storage software), and it will compete with "roomier SUV" results in search. Expect to rely on the logo and a distinctive spelling or suffix for protection.
- **Fit with the mark:** fine. Taking the shaving off the board leaves room.
- **Tagline that fits:** "Make your PC roomier."

### Not recommended, kept for reference

Shavely, Spale, Shive and Curlit all have a free `.app` and no direct hits, but each is weaker on meaning, pronunciation or sound-alikes.

## My recommendation

**Trimmel** if the priority is "can we actually own this name". **Roomier** if the priority is a name that explains itself. If neither feels right, I can run another round with a different idea (a made-up word with no meaning attached, or a two-word name).

## Before you commit: official checks (free, about an hour)

Do these for the finalist. I cannot run them from here because the databases need a browser.

1. **USPTO:** search the name at the USPTO trademark search site, in Classes 9 (downloadable software), 42 (software services) and 35. Search the name, the plural, and phonetic variants.
2. **EUIPO TMview** (covers the EU, UK and many other offices) and the **WIPO Global Brand Database**: the same name, all classes above.
3. **UK IPO** trademark search, since you may sell in the UK.
4. **Platforms:** Microsoft Store, GitHub, npm/PyPI (if we ever publish packages), and the social handles.
5. **Domains:** confirm with a registrar, then buy the `.com` or `.app` and a defensive pair.
6. If it all looks clear: a one-hour opinion from a trademark attorney, then file the application (a Class 9 and 42 filing in the US costs a few hundred dollars per class).
