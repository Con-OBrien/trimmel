Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

PC Optimizer is a free, open source Windows desktop app. It runs entirely on your computer. It has no servers or accounts of its own, and it does not collect, store or sell your data.

What the app does with Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks Google for the basic openid, email and profile information: your name, email address and profile picture. It uses these only to show who is signed in inside the app window.

  • This information stays on your computer. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • To keep you signed in, the app stores a Google refresh token in the Windows Credential Manager on your own account. Signing out deletes it.
  • The app does not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Other network activity

  • Drivers: when you ask it to, the app contacts Windows Update (Microsoft) to look for driver updates.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Files on your computer

Cleanup, Startup, Move apps and Drivers read and change files and settings on your PC, but only when you choose an action. Nothing from your PC is uploaded.

Removing your data

Sign out in the app to delete the stored token, or choose "Also delete my settings" when uninstalling. You can also revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions or concerns: please open an issue at github.com/Con-OBrien/pcoptimizer/issues.
