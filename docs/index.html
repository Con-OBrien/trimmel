Windows or Edge shows a warning?

The app is free and not code signed yet, so Windows does not know the publisher. This is expected for new, small apps.

  1. Edge: open the downloads list, click … beside the file, choose Keep, then Show more and Keep anyway.

Questions

 

Why does it need administrator rights?

Cleaning system folders and installing drivers are protected actions in Windows. The app asks once at launch.

Why do I have to sign in with Google?

It only shows your name in the app and keeps you signed in. It cannot read your email, Drive or any other Google data. See the privacy policy.

Is it safe to delete these files?

The app lists every item with its size first and only removes what you tick.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Settings, Apps, Installed apps, find PC Optimizer and choose Uninstall. The uninstaller can also remove your saved settings.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It is free and open source under the MIT licence.
