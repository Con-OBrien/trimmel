Trimmel vs Microsoft PC Manager vs BleachBit

Last reviewed 22 October 2026 · Windows 10 and 11

PC Manager is the simple, signed Microsoft option. BleachBit cleans the most apps and can shred files. Trimmel is for a full C: drive: it moves big programs to another drive and shows every change first.

All three are free and tidy a Windows PC, but they differ in focus. Here is how they compare, including where Trimmel is the weaker choice.

Which one to pick

  • Choose Microsoft PC Manager if you want something from Microsoft with a signed installer and nothing to think about.
  • Choose BleachBit if you want the deepest cleaning across many applications, secure deletion or scripting.
  • Choose Trimmel if your problem is a full C: drive and you want to move big programs to another drive, see exactly what will change before it does, and undo it.

Where Trimmel is weaker

  • It is new and small, so it has fewer cleaning targets than BleachBit.
  • The installer is not code signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. The download page explains how to proceed and shows the checksum.
  • It does not shred files or wipe free space.
