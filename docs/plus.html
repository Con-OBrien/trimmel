Trimmel Plus

Last reviewed 28 October 2026

Plus is an optional extra for £30, paid once. No subscription. Everything Trimmel does today stays free, with no time limit.

Get Plus

What stays free

Anything that keeps you safe is free and will not move behind a paywall:

  • Previews with sizes before anything is removed, and undo for startup changes and app moves.
  • The health score, the plain-English explanations and the protected-process list.
  • Cleanup, Startup, Storage, Apps, Old versions, Drivers scanning and moving apps back.
  • Security fixes and the source code of the free app (MIT licence).

What Plus adds

Plus charges for saving you time, not for the basics.

  • Scheduled cleanup: a weekly or monthly quiet clean of the safe items (temp files, browser and shader caches, error reports), with a note of what it freed. It never touches the Recycle Bin or Windows Update downloads.
  • Low-disk alerts: a notification when a drive runs low, and a weekly free-space summary.
  • Unlimited app moves: the free app lets you keep one app moved, or up to 10 GB in total. Plus removes that limit. Moving an app back is always free.

Deeper storage tools and a history of what Trimmel did are on the list for later. Tell me what you would like next.

How it works

  • Sign in with Google and pay on the account page. Payment is handled by Stripe, who also take care of tax and send your receipt. The price is £30, with tax shown at checkout.
  • You get a licence key, tied to your Google account. In Trimmel, sign in with the same account and paste the key into Settings, then Trimmel Plus. Trimmel checks the key on your PC without contacting a server, so it works offline.
  • One key covers up to three of your PCs and every update up to version 1.x.
  • A 14-day free trial of everything in Plus, which you start yourself in Settings. No card. When it ends, Trimmel returns to the free version. Nothing is deleted and nothing you changed is locked.
  • 30-day refund. Email support@trimmel.app and I will refund you. The key then stops showing on your account page.
  • If a key ever lapses, anything already moved or changed keeps working and can still be undone.
  • No nagging. No pop-ups, no fake scan results, no countdowns. Plus appears in one place, Settings.
  • Open source. The code stays public under the MIT licence. Plus is how the project pays for itself.
